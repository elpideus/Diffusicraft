Relief map

    Click a place for its coordinates and a /tp to copy. Hover to read them as you go.

    Zoomed out, the map shows the world's coarse map, which smooths the mountains, until you zoom in on a place: then it draws the land as the world generates it, and keeps it sharp when you zoom out again. A place seen for the first time takes a few seconds.

    Search for a biome by name, or type coordinates as x z.
